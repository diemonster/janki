# Blind Realtime `marin` audition

Within each numbered folder, listen to `A.wav` and `B.wav` before opening `run.json` or `answer-key.json`. Pick a winner for each pair, judging pronunciation first, then naturalness, pacing, clarity, and audio artifacts.

`01-yamanai` reuses the already-paid Realtime clips; every other pair is a fresh call with the same voice, instructions, PCM format, and sentence for both models.

1. `01-yamanai` — 雨、まだやまないの？
2. `02-fish-market` — この魚、魚市場で買ったんだ。
3. `03-whooping-cough` — この咳、百日咳かもしれない。
4. `04-temperature` — 体温、測ったの？三十八度もあるんだ。
5. `05-flight` — 夏休みに飛行機で北海道へ行くつもりです。

These are audition artifacts only. None replaces audio used by a deck.
